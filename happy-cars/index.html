About us

A used-car shop that people leave smiling.

Happy Cars started as a small washing and detailing bay. Customers kept asking us to help them buy and sell cars too, because they trusted how we treated theirs. So we built a showroom around that bay.

Today we buy, sell, finance and exchange pre-owned cars, and every car we sell passes through our own detailing team first. We price in the open, show the full inspection report, and handle the paperwork so you don't have to.

Our promise

  • No hidden charges. The price you see includes RC transfer.
  • Odometer and accident history verified on every car.
  • 7-day return if the car doesn't match its report.
  • Free first wash for every car we sell.
