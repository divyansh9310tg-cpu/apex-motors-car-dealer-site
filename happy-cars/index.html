How it works

From first look to your driveway.

    Inspected before it's listed

    Every car goes through our 140-point check. The condition score you see on each car comes from that report, not from a sales pitch.

  2. 02

    Year, kilometres, owners, insurance and accident history sit next to the price. Compare up to three cars side by side before you call anyone.

About us

A used-car showroom people leave smiling.

HS Cars started as a washing and detailing bay. Customers kept asking us to help them buy and sell cars too, because they trusted how we treated theirs, so we built a showroom around that bay.

Today we buy, sell, finance and exchange pre-owned cars. Every car passes through our own detailing team first, and every price is shown in the open.

  • Odometer and accident history verified on every car
  • The price you see includes RC transfer
  • Test drives at the showroom or at your door
